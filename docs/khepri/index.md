# Khepri

**Khepri is what happened when a colony plan became debris and the debris refused to die.**

Khepri is a civic-survival science fiction setting and strategic simulation focused on recovery, memory, maps, doctrine, and procedural compassion under pressure.

## Current Prototype

**CHAOS Coordinator Scaffold v0.5 — Useful Is Not Ready**

The first scripted proof arc is complete:

1. Tarsus Hull 3A — recover a place.
2. Evacuation Seven — recover Waiting.
3. Identity Drift — recover a name.
4. Quarantine Box Review — recover an unknown safely.
5. Foundry Decontamination — convert useful-but-not-clean recovery into safe delayed resource.
